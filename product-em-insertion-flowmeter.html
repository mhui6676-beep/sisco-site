产品说明

中文技术内容

1. 应用场景：用于既有管线上导电液体的流量计量与改造，探头直接插入管内测量，不需切断管路或加装法兰管段，适合不停产改造与不具备法兰条件的旧管线；覆盖污水、泥浆、矿浆、酸碱溶液、自来水与原水等场合。

2. 测量原理：依据法拉第电磁感应定律。导电介质切割磁场产生与平均流速成正比的感应电势，经转换器放大、滤波与运算后换算为流量；探头以插入管内的方式测得管内平均流速。

3. 适用管径范围：探头插入管内测量，可适配的管道口径范围与探头外径、插入深度（按常规规格，待核实）；具体口径需结合现场管道尺寸确认。

4. 公称压力等级：随所在管道的公称压力等级，需与探头承压能力匹配（具体范围待核实）。

5. 测介质与工况：电导率约 ≥ 5 μS/cm 的导电液体；不适用于油品、纯油、蒸汽等不导电介质。常规工作温度 −10 ~ +120 ℃（按常规规格，待核实）；安装需保证管道满管。

6. 材质：探头外壳与电极 316L / 钼 / 钽 / 哈氏合金 C 等，按介质腐蚀性匹配（具体配置待核实）。

7. 输出信号：4–20 mA（HART 可选）；RS485 / Modbus；脉冲与频率输出；继电器报警输出（均为可选或按型谱配置）。

8. 精度等级：插入式测量的精度与探头插入深度、满管条件及流速分布有关，具体精度等级与安装要求待核实；管道式电磁流量计常规可达 0.5 级（±0.5% 量程），高精度变送器可提供 0.3 级 / 0.25 级 / 0.2 级选项（具体能力与型号对应关系待核实）。

9. 适用标准与认证：CE 认证（公司已有电磁流量计 CE 证书，插入式是否覆盖及证书编号待核实）；ISO9001 质量管理体系认证（版本号待核实）。

10. 选型要点 / 用户常问 3 问

Q1：什么样的介质能测？必须是导电液体（电导率约 ≥ 5 μS/cm）；油、纯油、蒸汽等不导电介质不能测量。

Q2：什么时候选插入式，什么时候选管道式？既有管线不停产改造、不便加装法兰管段时选插入式；新敷设管道并需要长期稳定精度的场合优先选管道式电磁流量计。

Q3：安装要注意什么？探头应插入管内并保证满管，插入深度与安装方向需满足测量要求，避开弯头涡流区与底部积泥区（具体安装要求待核实）；转换器需要电源，供电方式按现场条件确定（待核实）。

English Technical Details

1. Application: Flow measurement and retrofit on existing pipelines carrying conductive liquids. The probe is inserted into the pipe, so the line does not have to be cut and no flanged pipe section is needed — a natural choice for tie-ins without production shutdown and for old lines without flanged connections. Covers wastewater, sludge, mineral slurry, acid and alkali solutions, raw and potable water.

2. Measuring principle: Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction. The conductive medium cutting the magnetic field generates an induced voltage proportional to the mean velocity; the transmitter amplifies, filters and processes this into a flow rate. The insertion probe measures the mean velocity in the pipe.

3. Applicable pipe size: The probe measures from inside the pipe; the applicable pipe size range, probe diameter and insertion depth are to be verified (standard practice, to be verified). The final size is confirmed against the actual pipe dimensions on site.

4. Nominal pressure rating: Follows the nominal pressure rating of the pipeline and must be matched against the pressure capability of the probe (range to be verified).

5. Measured medium and conditions: Conductive liquids of about 5 μS/cm conductivity or above; not applicable to oils, pure hydrocarbons, steam or other non-conductive media. Typical service −10 to +120 °C (standard practice, to be verified); the pipe must run full at the measuring point.

6. Materials: Probe housing and electrodes in 316L / molybdenum / tantalum / Hastelloy C, matched to the corrosiveness of the medium (exact configuration to be verified).

7. Output signal: 4–20 mA, with HART optional; RS485 / Modbus; pulse and frequency output; relay alarm output — all selectable per the type code.

8. Accuracy: Accuracy of an insertion measurement depends on insertion depth, full-bore condition and the velocity profile; the exact accuracy class and installation requirements are to be verified. The in-line electromagnetic flow meter is normally class 0.5 (±0.5% of span), with class 0.3 / 0.25 / 0.2 options for high-accuracy transmitters (exact capability and model mapping to be verified).

9. Standards and certification: CE certification (the company holds a CE certificate for the electromagnetic flow meter; whether it covers the insertion type and the certificate number are to be verified); ISO9001 quality management system certification (version on certificate to be verified).

10. Selection notes / three frequently asked questions

Q1: Which media can be measured? The medium must be conductive, about 5 μS/cm or above; oil, pure hydrocarbons and steam cannot be measured.

Q2: When to choose the insertion type and when the in-line type? Choose the insertion type for existing lines that must stay in service or where a flanged pipe section is impractical; for new pipelines needing long-term stable accuracy, choose the in-line electromagnetic flow meter.

Q3: What does installation involve? The probe is inserted into the pipe and the line must run full; insertion depth and orientation must meet the measuring requirements, and the mounting point should avoid the swirl zone behind elbows and sediment at the bottom of the pipe (exact installation requirements to be verified). The transmitter needs power; the supply arrangement follows the site conditions (to be verified).
